# Image Identity — Pablopvsky

Personal site for **Pablopvsky** (Pablo O.). The primary surface is a **typographic letter**, not a marketing collage. Imagery is optional and secondary.

## Style

- Quiet, editorial, paper-and-ink atmosphere
- Photographic or illustration work should feel like correspondence ephemera: soft daylight, matte paper grain, restrained contrast
- Prefer empty space and letterform over decorative objects

## Palette

- Schema: Aura Coral only. Seeds are accent `#f84657`, gray `#8c7375`, background `#1a131b`. No preset switcher.
- Canvas: Aura `gray-1` / `gray-2` (dark warm steps from that gray seed)
- Ink: Aura `gray-12` / `accent-12`
- Accent marks: Aura `accent-9` (`#f84657`) for links and emphasis only
- Avoid neon glow, purple SaaS gradients, and a second accent

## Composition / motifs

- Letter sheet, letterhead, signature line
- Asymmetry from written correspondence (left-aligned body, generous margins)
- Recurring motif: the custom **Pablopvsky** wordmark as the only brand mark

## Exclusions

- No centered keyvisual / mascot illustration as the hero
- No floating badges, promo chips, or sticker overlays
- No baked-in UI text or logos inside generated pixels
- No dark-mode neon / purple glow aesthetics
